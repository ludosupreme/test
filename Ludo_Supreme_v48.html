Rules
🎲 Roll a 6 to bring a pawn out of base.
🏃 Pawns must travel exactly to home (no overshoot).
⭐ Safe squares cannot be captured.
💥 Landing on an opponent captures their pawn (sent back to base).
🧱 Two or more opponent pawns on a square form a blockade.
🚫 A blockade cannot be passed or landed on by an opponent.
🎲 Dice Pool (default): a 6 gives an extra roll; all rolls go into one pool (e.g. 6, 6, 5) before any pawn moves.
🎲 Classic dice: each 6 grants another turn after you move.
🎯 Each die is used separately on any eligible pawn, in any order — split across pawns or stack on one. Captures and blockades are checked at every landing.
6️⃣ A 6 either brings a pawn out of base or moves a pawn 6. Dice 1–5 only move pawns already on the track or home lane.
🔄 Capture or finish earns a bonus roll, added to the end of the current pool.
⚠️ If a legal move exists you must play it; dice that cannot be used are forfeited.
❌ Three sixes in a row cancel the whole pool and end the turn.
🤝 Optional teams (alternate seats): Red+Yellow vs Green+Blue; in 6-player, Red+Orange+Yellow vs Green+Blue+Purple; in 8-player, Red+Orange+Yellow+Pink vs Green+Blue+Purple+Teal.
↩ Undo reverses the last human action. Yellow path = preview.
⬡ Six / Eight Players use a star board (78 / 104-square track), same rules, 4 pawns each. The 8-player board (and 6-player on phones) has triangle yards and one shared dice in the centre.
🏆 Ranked by finish order; game ends when one player/team remains.
