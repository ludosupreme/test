Ludo

Roll a 6 to leave base, race four pawns around the board and send rivals home.

2–8players
4pawns each

  • Classic board for 2 to 4 players, star boards for 6 and 8
  • Safe stars, blockades and exact-roll finish
  • Dice pool or classic dice
  • Optional teams, such as Red and Yellow against Green and Blue

Snakes & Ladders

Climb ladders, slide down snakes and be first to square 100.

100squares
3board layouts
1token each
  • Classic, Chaotic (more snakes) or Kids (more ladders) boards
  • Exact finish, or bounce back from 100
  • Share a square, or send opponents back to 1
  • Optional bonus squares: extra roll, or swap with the leader

Choose your rules

Every match starts with a rule set. Current is the app’s default. Standard follows the traditional game. Change any single option and it becomes Custom.
