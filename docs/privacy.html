Privacy Policy

App: Ludo Supreme (Android package com.ludosupreme.app) · Developer: Shahjahan Kabir · Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains how Ludo Supreme (“the app”), which includes the Ludo and Snakes & Ladders games, handles information. In short: the app does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal data.

1. Summary

  • No account, sign-up or login is required.
  • No advertising and no ad SDKs.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • No personal information is sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • Game data is stored only on your device.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access, and the app does not read your advertising ID or device identifiers.

3. Information stored on your device

To make the games work, the app saves the following locally on your device (in the app’s own storage). It is never uploaded by us:

DataPurpose
Player names you type (for example “Red”)Show names during a match
Game settings (rule set, dice mode, AI difficulty, sound, music, language, colour-blind and vibration options)Remember your preferences
Saved game in progress and move historyLet you pause, resume and undo
Statistics (games played, wins, captures, sixes, badges)Show your progress

You can remove this data at any time by using Reset Statistics in the app, clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings, or uninstalling the app.

4. Permissions

  • Vibration — optional haptic feedback during play. It can be switched off in Settings.
  • Notifications — optional “Game reminders” (a notification a number of hours after you stop playing) created on your device. The app asks before enabling them and you can turn them off in the app or in Android Settings. Reminders are scheduled locally; no notification service or server is involved.

5. Sharing a result

The app has a Share button that creates an image or text of your result and opens Android’s standard share sheet. You decide whether to share it and with which app. The image is created on your device and held temporarily in the app’s cache. We do not receive it. Anything you share is then governed by the privacy policy of the app you choose.

6. Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or social-media SDKs, and does not load content from external servers during play. The app is distributed through Google Play, which processes your data under Google’s Privacy Policy; we do not control that processing.

7. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It contains no ads, in-app purchases, chat or user-generated content. Because nothing is collected, there is no personal information from children to disclose or delete.

8. Data security and retention

Because data stays on your device, it is protected by your device’s own security (screen lock, encryption). We keep no copies and have no server-side retention. Data is deleted when you reset statistics, clear app storage or uninstall.

9. Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. If you are in a region with data-protection rights (such as the EU/UK GDPR or California CCPA) and have a question, contact us using the details below.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change (for example, if a feature that uses the internet is added), we will update this page and the “Last updated” date before the change is released.
