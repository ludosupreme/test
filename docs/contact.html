Frequently asked

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Both games are played on your device.

How do I delete my data?

Use Reset Statistics in the app, clear the app’s storage in Android Settings, or uninstall it. See the Privacy Policy.

What do “Current” and “Standard” rules mean?

Current is the app’s default rule set. Standard follows traditional rules. You can also build a Custom rule set in the match setup screen.
