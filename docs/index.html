Rules at a glance

Ludo

Roll a 6 to bring a pawn out. Land on a lone opponent to send it back to base. Start squares and ★ squares are safe. Reach home with an exact roll. The first player (or team) to bring all pawns home wins.

Snakes & Ladders

Every token starts on square 1. Ladders move you up, snakes slide you down. Reach square 100 first — by default you need the exact roll to finish.

Rule sets

Current uses the app’s default rules (dice pool, blockades, bonus rolls). Standard uses traditional rules (classic dice, no blockades, a bonus roll only from a 6, three 6s forfeit the turn). Choose Custom to mix options.
